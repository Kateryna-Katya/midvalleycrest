About the Game

Sounds easy enough, right? Wrong.

Midvalley Crest: Cargo Dash is built around a deceptively simple challenge — transport goods from one point to another. However, the terrain is never stable. Slopes, bumps, and sudden drops constantly threaten to spill the cargo.
Players quickly learn that speed alone isn’t enough.
Smooth movement, controlled acceleration, and careful handling are key to success.

Gameplay Loop

Short runs, constant retries

Each level is designed as a quick run where players attempt to deliver as much cargo as possible.
Even small mistakes can result in lost items, encouraging players to retry and improve performance.
Every time you crash (and you will crash), you'll think 'okay, just one more try' and suddenly it's 2 AM.

Core Mechanics

Fast deliveries across unstable terrain

It's all about finding that sweet spot between going fast enough to be fun and slow enough to not dump everything.
Push a little too hard and the whole run unravels, but dial it in just right and every bump becomes part of your rhythm.

  • Balance and movement control

  • Drive across uneven terrain

  • Adjust speed on slopes and drops

  • Prevent cargo from falling off

  • Maintain balance to keep cargo stable

  • Reach delivery zones with maximum load
